Frequently Asked Questions

Does HashPaper ever see or store my confidential work? +

No. Your text is converted into a 64-character SHA-256 hexadecimal hash inside your browser using the Web Crypto API before any network request occurs. HashPaper only receives and timestamps the hash.

Why must I keep the downloaded .txt reference file unaltered? +

SHA-256 is an avalanche cryptographic function—changing even a single space, comma, or line break produces a completely different 64-character hash. Keeping your exact downloaded UTF-8 .txt file guarantees 100% reproducible verification anywhere in the world.

Is a HashPaper timestamp a replacement for Copyright or Patent registration? +

No. HashPaper provides verifiable cryptographic proof of prior possession and timestamped existence to strengthen evidentiary records under BSA Section 63, complementing formal statutory registrations under the Copyright Act, 1957 or Patents Act, 1970.
