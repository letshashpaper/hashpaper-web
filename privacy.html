Privacy Policy & Zero-Retention Architecture

How HashPaper protects your intellectual property confidentiality and personal data in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

1. Zero-Knowledge Processing of Intellectual Property

HashPaper is engineered so that your confidential text (scripts, source code, manuscripts, lyrics, or business proposals) never leaves your web browser. All SHA-256 hashing happens locally on your device via the W3C Web Crypto API. HashPaper servers never receive, cache, log, or view your intellectual property content.

2. Personal Data We Collect & Purpose

To issue an evidentiary Priority Certificate and process payments, we collect only the minimum necessary metadata:

  • Declarant Legal Name & Email Address: Used exclusively to populate your private Priority Certificate and dispatch it via transactional email.
  • Asset Title & Category: Recorded on your private certificate metadata to identify the timestamped work.
  • SHA-256 Digest & Byte Length: Anchored to our database in Mumbai, India (ap-south-1) and submitted to public OpenTimestamps calendar servers.
  • Payment Audit References: Razorpay Order ID and Payment ID stored strictly for financial reconciliation and anti-fraud verification (we never store card or UPI credentials).

3. Blind Public Ledger Masking

To prevent public scraping of personal identities or confidential project titles, our public verification endpoint (/verify.html) queries an isolated ledger table that exposes only the Certificate ID, SHA-256 hash, asset classification, IST timestamp, and masked declarant initials (e.g., M*** D*** N*** K***). Your email address and document title are never published publicly.

4. Sub-Processors & Data Localization

Primary certificate records are hosted on encrypted PostgreSQL infrastructure located in Mumbai, India (ap-south-1). We utilize Cloudflare (edge delivery), Razorpay (PCI-DSS compliant Indian payment processing), Resend (transactional certificate email dispatch), and OpenTimestamps (public cryptographic digest anchoring). We do not sell, rent, or monetize user data or run third-party advertising trackers.

5. Data Principal Rights & Grievance Officer

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may request access to or correction of your private certificate contact metadata (excluding immutable ledger hashes and timestamps required for evidentiary integrity) by writing to our Grievance Officer at legal@hashpaper.in.
