Governing access to and use of HashPaper (operated by Your Legal Indian / MDM Global Holdings, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India).

1. Nature of Cryptographic Timestamping Service

HashPaper provides an automated, zero-knowledge cryptographic priority timestamping utility. When you enter text into HashPaper, your web browser computes a 64-character SHA-256 hexadecimal digest locally on your device. HashPaper records that digest, your declarant metadata, an authoritative Indian Standard Time (IST) timestamp, and an OpenTimestamps anchor, and issues a QR-verifiable Priority Certificate.

2. Zero-Retention & User Custody of Reference File

HashPaper never receives, inspects, or stores your underlying text or intellectual property payload. Because a SHA-256 hash is mathematically irreversible, HashPaper cannot recover lost documents. You are solely responsible for preserving the exact, unaltered UTF-8 .txt reference file downloaded by your browser upon certificate minting. Modifying even a single character or space in that file will prevent future hash matching.

3. No Statutory IP Grant or Legal Representation

A HashPaper Priority Certificate constitutes electronic evidence of prior possession and timestamped existence aligned with Section 63 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA), 2023. It is not a statutory copyright, patent, or trademark registration certificate issued by the Government of India, nor does use of HashPaper create an advocate-client relationship.

4. Prohibited Submissions

You agree not to submit hashes corresponding to unlawful content, malware, child sexual abuse material, or content infringing third-party statutory rights. While HashPaper operates a blind hash ledger, we reserve the right to comply with lawful orders issued by competent Indian courts or statutory authorities.
