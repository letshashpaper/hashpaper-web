1. Scope of Service

HashPaper provides a browser-based cryptographic timestamping utility that computes a deterministic SHA-256 hash of user-supplied text on the client device and records that 64-character hexadecimal digest alongside an authoritative Indian Standard Time (IST) timestamp and an OpenTimestamps ledger anchor.

2. Zero-Retention & User Custody Responsibility

Because HashPaper operates a strict zero-retention architecture, HashPaper never receives, stores, or can recover your original text or file. Upon certificate minting, your browser automatically downloads an unaltered UTF-8 .txt reference file. You are solely responsible for backing up and preserving that exact file unaltered; modifying even a single character or space will prevent future SHA-256 match verification.
